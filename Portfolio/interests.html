Music is something I've explored through my classes. I've worked with different sounds and learned how they can change the mood of a project. I also enjoy experimenting with audio and putting different sounds together.

Other Hobbies

I've also become interested in movies and how sound affects different scenes. After working on a horror sound design project, I started paying more attention to how sound effects are used in movies.

One term I learned in sound design was stinger, which is a short sound used to emphasize a sudden moment. I found it especially interesting how a small sound could change the mood of a scene.
